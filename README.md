# DreamScaler

**See your next note.**

Explore scales, visualize chord progressions and bring colour to your keyboard.
DreamScaler combines a standalone desktop app with an RGBW LED strip to make
musical patterns visible, on screen and on your keys.

[Explore DreamScaler](https://tomasmark79.github.io/DreamScalerWeb/)

![DreamScaler lighting up a keyboard](assets/dreamscaler-in-action.png)

## A new way to explore music

- **Scales:** explore 44 scales in any root, with colour-coded notes and a live keyboard preview.
- **Chords:** visualize progressions, adjust the tempo or move through each chord manually.
- **Light playground:** explore six effects and create your own RGBW colour.
- **Your instrument:** choose a profile, align the strip and test individual LEDs. Create custom layouts with editable profiles.
- **Your preferences:** dark and light themes, English / Czech / German, and sharp or flat note names.

The standalone app is built in Python and designed for Windows, macOS and Linux.
You can explore its on-screen preview without connecting the LED strip.

## Why I made it

As an adult studying music theory, I struggle to remember scales. But to me,
scales are a gateway to the magic of human feelings and emotions. I created
DreamScaler to make those patterns visible and help shape the mood of my own music.

— Tomas Mark

## Bring it into Bitwig Studio

The optional Bitwig extension connects the LED strip to a MIDI channel on a track
of your choice, giving you visual feedback from the actual notes playing in your DAW.
It is available with the kit bundle and requires Bitwig Studio.

(A Max for Live version for Ableton Live is currently in development.)
